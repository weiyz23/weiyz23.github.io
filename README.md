# Yunze Wei (Rixton) · 卫蕴泽

[Personal homepage](https://weiyz23.github.io/) · [GitHub](https://github.com/weiyz23)

I am a Ph.D. student at the Institute for Network Sciences and Cyberspace, Tsinghua University, advised by [Prof. Xia Yin](https://www.cs.tsinghua.edu.cn/csen/info/1309/4354.htm), with research guidance from [Assoc. Researcher Xingang Shi](https://www.insc.tsinghua.edu.cn/inscen/info/1255/1087.htm). I received my Bachelor of Engineering from the Department of Computer Science and Technology, Tsinghua University, in 2023.

My research applies mathematical modeling, representation learning, and multi-agent collaboration to Internet measurement, observability, cyberspace mapping, and routing security. My work includes BGP anomaly detection, Looking Glass service discovery, and router geolocation. I also have experience in knowledge graphs, graph computing, and industrial control and IoT security, including contributing to AtlasGraph’s multi-machine cluster framework in Rust.
